# Day 17 — Interactive 3D storefront demonstration

Four real, source-backed tenants are represented by **original illustrative interactive signboards** in the fictional corridor. Their labels link to the shared retail data model. Clicking a signboard shows a business information card; selecting a store in the 2D directory also populates the 3D card.

**Critical:** The signboard positions are deliberately fabricated. Neither the corridor nor the tenant positions match the Royal Bank Plaza floor plan. Do not imply verified placement or indoor directions.

## Manual test
1. Open **3D demo** and walk toward the bay signs near the starting end.
2. Click Mos Mos Coffee or Tim Hortons; the selected store details should appear.
3. Tab to a 3D signboard and press Enter; keyboard interaction must work.
4. Close the card, then choose a store from the 2D directory and return to 3D; confirm the selection persists.
5. Move into the walls; collision bounds still work.
