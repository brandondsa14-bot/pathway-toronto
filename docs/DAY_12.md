# Day 12 — Optimized reusable demonstration corridor

The 3D demo now shows a fictional corridor built from reusable geometry: floor, end walls, ceiling, storefront-like glass bays, lighting panels, and a highlighted synthetic floor strip. These are **original primitives** generated in code, without outside 3D assets or trademarks. Geometry is illustrative and not verified against any building floor plan.
